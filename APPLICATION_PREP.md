# Claude Startups — honest application prep

Official entry: https://claude.com/programs/startups
Official terms: https://www.anthropic.com/startup-program-official-terms

As observed October 8, 2026, the startup page describes 1 year of Claude Team (up to five Premium seats if new to Team), $1,000 Claude API credits and partner offers. Its FAQ states startups founded within 5 years or funded within 2 years, a Claude Console account, a company email matching the site domain, and a short description of what's being built. Program terms give Anthropic discretion over eligibility and evaluation. Benefits and criteria may change.

## Before you submit

1. Ensure your business activity genuinely fits a startup project (don't invent registration/funding/customers if absent).
2. Check the program terms and local supportability rules.
3. Confirm `anhdt@ahsolutions.tech` is able to RECEIVE verification and follow-up emails.
4. Ensure `https://ahsolutions.tech` loads and the product text agrees with the application.
5. Create/log into Claude Console at https://platform.claude.com/ with the corporate email.
6. Prepare a truthful short description covering problem, intended users, product, current stage, actual AI role, and near-term milestones.
7. Do not describe a Claude API integration unless one genuinely exists.
8. Apply from the official page. Anthropic can approve quickly or manually review; no acceptance guarantee.
9. If accepted, read the offer and redemption details before starting a paid Team subscription. Regular Team plans have separate seat/minimum-member and billing requirements.
