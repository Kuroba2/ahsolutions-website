AH BRIEFFLOW / Interactive Demo

Turn a client brief into a clearer starting point.

Paste your own software project brief. This prototype categorizes candidate statements, suggests clarification questions, and assembles an editable proposal outline. It is a limited rules-based baseline.

What this is: A working rule-based browser prototype, not a Claude-powered agent. Brief text is processed locally by this page’s JavaScript and is not submitted to an AH Solutions backend or AI provider. Normal requests for website assets and hosting analytics may still occur. Outputs need human review.
